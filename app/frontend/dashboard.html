Key findings

Each figure comes from the SQL analysis and also appears in the Power BI dashboard. They describe patterns in historical data, not causes.

8.07%

Historical default rate

24,825 of 307,511 applicants defaulted, about 1 in 12. This is the baseline for every comparison.

18.84%

Lowest external credit scores

Applicants with external score 2 below 0.2 defaulted at 18.84%, against 4.57% for scores of 0.6 to 0.8. This was the clearest split in the SQL analysis.

11.46%

Younger applicants

Applicants under 30 defaulted at 11.46%, against 4.92% for those aged 60 and above. The rate fell at every age step.

17.15%

Occupation

Low-skill Laborers had the highest rate, but it is a small group of 2,093 applicants. Accountants had the lowest (4.83%). Laborers account for the most defaulters overall, 23.52% of all.

5.95%

Income

Applicants in the 300,000 and above band defaulted at 5.95%. There was no steady pattern below that, and the highest rate was 8.62% for 100,000 to 149,999.

These are associations in historical data, not causes. Occupation, age and income should not be used on their own to approve or reject an applicant.

Decision-support tool only. The model was trained on historical data and shows associations, not causes. It uses information that this site does not collect, including gender, which is filled with a typical value. Fields left blank are also filled with typical values. Do not use it as the only basis for a credit decision.
