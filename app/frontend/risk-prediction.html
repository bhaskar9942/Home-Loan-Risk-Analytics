How to read the result

Estimated probability. The model's estimate of default risk, based on how similar applicants behaved in historical data.

Review threshold (10%). Estimates at or above 10% are marked "Above the review threshold". This flags a case for a closer look. It is not a decision.

Reliability. It depends on how many external scores are entered. With all three it is High. With fewer, a warning explains what testing showed. With none, no risk label is shown.

Decision-support tool only. The model was trained on historical data and shows associations, not causes. It uses information that this site does not collect, including gender, which is filled with a typical value. Fields left blank are also filled with typical values. Do not use it as the only basis for a credit decision.
